# Day 1: Fetch & Async/Await

[Day contents](README.md) · [Week overview](../README.md)

## Learning Summary

Use Fetch and async/await to structure network operations and handle failures. Compare the syntax with promise chains and coordinate independent requests with Promise.all.

## Skills to Practice

- Read a response and extract its data.
- Use try/catch around asynchronous operations.
- Combine multiple promise results.
