# Visible question — social awareness and relationships

**Q1: Empathy means:**

**C. Understanding others' emotions and perspectives.**

Empathy involves trying to understand another person's experience. It does not require agreeing with every opinion (A), feeling pity (B), or avoiding necessary disagreements (D). A constructive response can acknowledge someone's feelings while still discussing a different view or a boundary.
