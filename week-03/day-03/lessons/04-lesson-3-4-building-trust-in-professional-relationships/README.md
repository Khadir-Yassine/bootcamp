## Lesson 3.4 – Building Trust in Professional Relationships

Trust is the foundation of all professional relationships.

Trust is built through:

- consistency

- emotional stability

- clear communication

- respect

- reliability

Trust is destroyed by:

- emotional outbursts

- inconsistency

- lack of transparency

- blaming others

- ignoring feedback

In teams, trust allows:

- open communication

- faster collaboration

- constructive conflict

- higher performance

📝 **Reflection**
Ask yourself:

- Do people feel safe speaking honestly with me?

- How do I react when I receive feedback?
