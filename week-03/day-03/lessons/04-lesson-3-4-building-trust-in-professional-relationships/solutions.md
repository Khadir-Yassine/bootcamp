# Building trust — worked reflection

Illustrative reflection; adapt it to your own behavior.

**Do people feel safe speaking honestly with me?** I cannot know without asking. In this example I invite contrary views in reviews, thank the person who identifies a risk, and avoid punishing someone for raising a concern. I can ask a teammate privately: “Is there anything in my reactions that makes it harder to disagree with me?”

**How do I react when I receive feedback?** My first impulse may be to justify the decision. A constructive sequence is to pause, summarize what I heard, ask for an example, agree on a concrete next step, and report back after trying it. For example: “You noticed I interrupt in planning. I will wait until the speaker finishes and ask one clarifying question before proposing a solution. Can we check in after the next two meetings?”
