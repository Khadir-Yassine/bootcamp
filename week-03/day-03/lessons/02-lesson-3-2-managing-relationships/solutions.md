# Managing relationships — worked disagreement

Illustrative team scenario, not a fabricated personal memory.

**Situation:** A teammate missed a delivery date and I publicly said they were unreliable.

1. **What might they have been feeling?** Overwhelmed, embarrassed, or defensive. These are possibilities to explore, not facts I can infer from a missed deadline.
2. **What pressures might they have had?** An unclear requirement, an unexpected production incident, competing deadlines, or a personal responsibility. I should ask privately rather than assume.
3. **How could I respond differently?** “The integration depends on this change, and we missed today's handoff. What blocked you? Can we agree on a smaller deliverable by tomorrow, and flag risks earlier?” This states an observable impact, invites context, and ends with an achievable agreement.

Follow up on the agreed action without attaching a character judgment to the person.
