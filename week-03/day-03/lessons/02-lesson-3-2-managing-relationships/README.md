## Lesson 3.2 – Empathy: Understanding Before Reacting

Empathy is the ability to **understand what another person is feeling and why**, without necessarily agreeing with them.

Empathy is not:

- weakness

- submission

- accepting bad behavior

Empathy is:

- awareness

- emotional intelligence

- maturity

There are two main forms of empathy:

- **Cognitive empathy**: understanding someone’s perspective

- **Emotional empathy**: sensing someone’s emotional state

Example:

- A teammate is late on a task
Instead of thinking: “They are irresponsible”
An empathetic response asks: “What might be happening on their side?”

Empathy helps you:

- de-escalate tension

- communicate more clearly

- respond instead of reacting

📝 **Exercise**
Think of a recent disagreement and answer:

- What was the other person possibly feeling?

- What pressures might they have had?

- How could I have responded differently?
