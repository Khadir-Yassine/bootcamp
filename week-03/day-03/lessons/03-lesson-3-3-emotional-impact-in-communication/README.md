## Lesson 3.3 – Emotional Impact in Communication

Every interaction creates an **emotional impact**, whether intentional or not.

Your words, tone, posture, and reactions can:

- motivate

- discourage

- reassure

- intimidate

Professionals with high emotional intelligence:

- are aware of the emotional impact they create

- take responsibility for it

- adjust when necessary

Key idea:
👉 **Intent does not cancel impact**.

You may not intend to offend, but if the impact is negative, the relationship suffers.
