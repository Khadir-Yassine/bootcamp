# Lesson 3.6 – Relationship Management as a Career Skill

Course source: [Geeks Institute](https://www.geeks.institute/).
## Lesson 3.6 – Relationship Management as a Career Skill

Relationship management is not optional.

It directly affects:

- employability

- leadership potential

- team effectiveness

- career progression

People are more likely to:

- work with you

- support you

- recommend you

when they feel:

- respected

- understood

- emotionally safe
