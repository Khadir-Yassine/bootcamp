Course source: [Geeks Institute](https://www.geeks.institute/).
## Lesson 3.5 – Managing Conflict Constructively

Conflict is normal and unavoidable.

The problem is not conflict itself, but **how it is handled**.

Unhealthy conflict behaviors:

- attacking the person

- raising your voice

- shutting down communication

- avoiding responsibility

Healthy conflict management includes:

- focusing on facts, not personalities

- expressing emotions calmly

- listening actively

- seeking solutions, not winners

Emotionally intelligent professionals use conflict to:

- clarify misunderstandings

- improve processes

- strengthen relationships
