## **MODULE 3 – Empathy and Relationships**

### Lesson 3.1 – What Is Social Awareness?

Social awareness is the ability to **read the emotional and social environment** around you.

It includes:

- understanding others’ emotions

- noticing non-verbal signals

- recognizing group dynamics

- adapting your behavior to the context

In professional life, social awareness allows you to:

- communicate more effectively

- avoid unnecessary conflicts

- understand unspoken expectations

- work better in teams

A socially aware person does not focus only on what is said, but also on:

- how it is said

- when it is said

- by whom it is said

- in which context it is said
