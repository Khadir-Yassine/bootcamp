# Day 3: SOFT SKILLS: part 3

[Day contents](README.md) · [Week overview](../README.md)

## Learning Summary

Develop empathy, social awareness, trust and constructive conflict management. Consider how emotional impact and communication choices affect collaboration.

## Skills to Practice

- Recognize another person’s perspective.
- Build trust through consistent communication.
- Handle disagreements constructively.

- Lesson 3.6 – Relationship Management as a Career Skill
