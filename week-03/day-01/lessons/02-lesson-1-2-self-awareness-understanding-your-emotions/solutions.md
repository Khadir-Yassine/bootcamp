# Self-awareness — worked reflection

This is an illustrative workplace scenario, not a claim about the repository owner's experiences. Personal reflection has no single correct answer.

| Prompt | Example response |
| --- | --- |
| What triggered a strong emotion today? | A teammate identified a bug in my pull request during a group review. I felt embarrassed. |
| What did I think in that moment? | “Everyone thinks I am not good enough.” That was an interpretation, not something anyone said. |
| How did I react? | I interrupted the reviewer and began defending my implementation before understanding the bug. |
| What could I do differently next time? | Pause, name the feeling privately, ask for a failing example, thank the reviewer, and agree on the fix. |

The useful distinction is between the observable event, the thought attached to it, the emotion, and the chosen response. Replace the example with an honest observation of your own day when practicing.
