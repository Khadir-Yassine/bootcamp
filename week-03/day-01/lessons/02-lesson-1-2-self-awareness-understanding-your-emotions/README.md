# Lesson 1.2 – Self-Awareness: Understanding Your Emotions

Course source: [Geeks Institute](https://www.geeks.institute/).
### Lesson 1.2 – Self-Awareness: The Foundation of EQ

Self-awareness means being able to **observe your emotions without being controlled by them**.

Most emotional reactions follow this chain:

**Situation → Interpretation → Emotion → Reaction**

Important point:
👉 You do not react to the situation itself.
👉 You react to **your interpretation** of the situation.

Example:

- Situation: A colleague does not reply to your message

- Interpretation: “They are ignoring me”

- Emotion: Frustration

- Reaction: Passive-aggressive behavior

Another person might interpret the same situation differently and react calmly.

Developing self-awareness allows you to:

- catch emotional reactions early

- reduce impulsive behavior

- make better decisions

📝 **Exercise**
At the end of the day, answer:

- What triggered a strong emotion today?

- What did I think in that moment?

- How did I react?

- What could I do differently next time?
