### Lesson 1.3 – Emotional Regulation & Self-Control

Emotional balance does **not** mean:

- Suppressing emotions

- Ignoring feelings

It means:

- Recognizing emotions

- Managing them intentionally

There is always a **space between stimulus and response**.
Maturity means **expanding that space**.

**Tools to regain control**:

- Name the emotion (“I am frustrated”)

- Take perspective (“Is there another explanation?”)

- Pause before acting

- Focus on breathing and presence

🧠 This ability improves with practice due to **neuroplasticity**.
