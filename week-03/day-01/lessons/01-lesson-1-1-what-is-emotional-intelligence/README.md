## **MODULE 1 – Emotional Intelligence (EQ)**

Lesson 1.1 – What is Emotional Intelligence?

Emotional Intelligence (EQ) is the ability to **recognize, understand, manage, and use emotions effectively**, both your own emotions and those of others.

In professional life, people rarely fail because of lack of knowledge.
They fail because of:

- poor emotional control

- miscommunication

- inability to work with others

- difficulty handling pressure or feedback

Emotional Intelligence helps you:

- stay calm under stress

- communicate clearly

- adapt to change

- build strong professional relationships

EQ is structured into **four main domains**:

1. **Self-awareness** – understanding what you feel and why

2. **Self-management** – controlling reactions and impulses

3. **Social awareness** – understanding others’ emotions

4. **Relationship management** – interacting effectively with people
