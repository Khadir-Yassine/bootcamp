# Lesson 1.4 – Building Emotional Resilience

Course source: [Geeks Institute](https://www.geeks.institute/).
### Lesson 1.4 – Building Emotional Resilience

Resilience is the ability to:

- stay stable under pressure

- recover after setbacks

- adapt emotionally to change

Resilient people:

- do not deny emotions

- do not explode emotionally

- learn from difficult situations

To build resilience:

1. Identify recurring emotional triggers

2. Prepare a response in advance

3. Practice that response consistently

Over time, emotional balance becomes a habit.
