# Day 1: SOFT SKILLS: part 1

[Day contents](README.md) · [Week overview](../README.md)

## Learning Summary

Recognize emotions, understand their triggers, regulate reactions and build resilience. Apply these ideas to feedback, pressure and professional relationships.

## Skills to Practice

- Distinguish self-awareness from self-management.
- Identify emotional triggers before reacting.
- Choose constructive responses to pressure and feedback.

- MODULE 1 – Emotional Intelligence (EQ) Lesson 1.1 – What is Emotional Intelligence?
- Lesson 1.2 – Self-Awareness: The Foundation of EQ
- Lesson 1.3 – Emotional Regulation & Self-Control
- Lesson 1.4 – Building Emotional Resilience
