# Week 3 Learning Summary

[Week contents](README.md) · [All weeks](../CONTENTS.md)

## Weekly Focus

Combine emotional intelligence and communication skills with asynchronous JavaScript. Move from callbacks and promises to API-based application projects.

## Day-by-Day Learning

### [Day 1: SOFT SKILLS: part 1](day-01/summary.md)

Recognize emotions, understand their triggers, regulate reactions and build resilience. Apply these ideas to feedback, pressure and professional relationships.

### [Day 2: SOFT SKILLS: part 2](day-02/summary.md)

Study how messages are interpreted, including non-verbal signals and communication filters. Ask clearer questions and check understanding to reduce avoidable misunderstandings.

### [Day 3: SOFT SKILLS: part 3](day-03/summary.md)

Develop empathy, social awareness, trust and constructive conflict management. Consider how emotional impact and communication choices affect collaboration.

### [Day 4: Asynchronous Javascript](day-04/summary.md)

Compare synchronous and asynchronous execution, understand callback nesting, and use promises to organize operations that complete later. Follow both successful and failed execution paths.

### [Day 5: Mini-Project Day](day-05/summary.md)

Use external APIs in the Star Wars, Pokedex and Currency Converter applications. Connect network results to the interface and account for waiting, missing data and errors.
