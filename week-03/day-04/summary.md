## Learning Summary

Compare synchronous and asynchronous execution, understand callback nesting, and use promises to organize operations that complete later. Follow both successful and failed execution paths.

## Skills to Practice

- Explain why asynchronous results are not immediately available.
- Chain promises and handle rejected operations.
- Read and improve callback-based control flow.
