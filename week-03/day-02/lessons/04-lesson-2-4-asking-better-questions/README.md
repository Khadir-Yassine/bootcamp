# Lesson 2.4 – Asking Better Questions

Course source: [Geeks Institute](https://www.geeks.institute/).
### Lesson 2.4 – Asking Better Questions

### 1. Why Questions Matter

Questions shape conversations and influence understanding. Asking before reacting helps replace assumptions with clarity and turns discussions into constructive exchanges rather than conflicts.

---

### 2. What Makes a Question Effective

Effective questions help to:

- **Clarify meaning**

- **Reduce assumptions**

- **Encourage reflection**

Examples:

- “Can you give an example?”

- “What do you mean exactly?”

- “What is the impact of this?”

---

### 3. Types of Useful Questions

a. Open Questions

Open questions encourage deeper answers and exploration.
Examples: “How?”, “Why?”

b. Clarifying Questions

They ensure understanding and avoid misunderstandings.
Example: “What do you mean exactly?”

c. Reflective Questions

They promote self-analysis and critical thinking.
Example: “What made you think that?”

---

### 4. Impact of Asking Better Questions

Asking good questions:

- **Reduces conflict**

- **Improves understanding**

- **Builds trust**

People feel heard, respected, and more open to collaboration.

---

### 5. Key Takeaway

Good communicators ask before they react. Asking better questions strengthens communication in professional, academic, and personal settings.
