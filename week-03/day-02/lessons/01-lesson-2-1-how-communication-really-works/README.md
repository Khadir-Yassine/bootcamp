## **MODULE 2 – Communication Skills**

Lesson 2.1 – How Communication Really Works

Communication is not only about **what you say**, but **how you say it**.

Research shows:

- Words represent only a small part of communication:

- Words: 15%

- Voice (tone, rhythm): 35%

- Body language: 50%

If your words say “I’m fine” but your body says the opposite, people believe the body.

👉 Good communication requires:

- clarity

- coherence

- emotional awareness
