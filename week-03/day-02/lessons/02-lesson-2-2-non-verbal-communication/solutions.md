# Non-verbal communication — worked observation

Illustrative example; this does not report an actual conversation by the repository owner.

- **Gestures and message:** I said “I am listening,” while checking my phone and turning away. Those signals contradicted the words. A better response is to put the phone aside, turn toward the speaker, and avoid interrupting.
- **Tone:** My fast, clipped delivery sounded tense even though I intended to be helpful. I could slow down, lower my volume, and ask whether the other person wants suggestions or simply to be heard.

Check understanding directly: “Did my explanation come across clearly?” Eye contact and gestures differ across cultures and individuals; ask instead of treating one gesture as proof of someone's intent.
