Course source: [Geeks Institute](https://www.geeks.institute/).
### Lesson 2.2 – Non-Verbal Communication

Non-verbal communication includes:

- posture and body orientation

- facial expressions

- eye contact

- gestures

- voice tone and rhythm

- physical distance

Examples:

- Open posture → openness and confidence

- Closed posture → defensiveness

- Calm voice → credibility

- Fast or shaky voice → stress or insecurity

📝 **Exercise**
Observe yourself in a conversation:

- Are your gestures aligned with your message?

- Is your tone calm or tense?
