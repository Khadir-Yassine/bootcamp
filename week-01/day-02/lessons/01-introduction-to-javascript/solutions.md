## Exercise 1: Address Variables

The three variables are concatenated into one sentence. The example prints `I live in 55 av Bosquet, in France`. The HTML demo links a separate JavaScript file and displays the sentence through `textContent`.

## Exercise 2: Future Age

Subtract the birth year from the future year. With 2000 and 2030, the person turns 30 in 2030 and can be 29 or 30 during that year, depending on whether the birthday has passed. These are illustrative years, not personal information.
