## Exercise 1: Odd and Even Numbers

The inclusive condition `i <= 15` prints all 16 requested values, starting with `0 is even` and ending with `15 is odd`. The browser demo displays every message on the page.

## Exercise 2: Filtering Versus Stopping

The first loop uses `continue` to skip nonstrings and prints `John`, `Sarah`, `Rudolf`. Capitalizing a first letter does not alter the remaining letters. The second loop works from the original input and uses `break`, producing only `john`, `sarah` before encountering 23.
