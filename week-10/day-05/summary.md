# Day 5: OOP Inheritance, Encapsulation, Polymorphism and Multiple Inheritance

[Day contents](README.md) · [Week overview](../README.md)

## Learning Summary

Study inheritance, encapsulation, polymorphism, class/static methods, properties and dunder methods. Modules, exceptions and the Pagination challenge extend the ideas into reusable programs.

## Skills to Practice

- Choose appropriate inheritance and method types.
- Organize code into modules.
- Implement special behavior and handle exceptional cases.
