## 1. Three import styles

operators.py defines addOperator and divideOperator. calculator.py demonstrates importing the whole module, importing the two functions directly, and importing a module alias. Each style prints **10 and 4.0** for operands 8 and 2. Run `python calculator.py`. Standard division by zero raises ZeroDivisionError.
