## 1. Letter indexes

Use enumerate to pair every character with its zero-based position, then append positions to each letter’s list. dodo gives **{d:[0,2],o:[1,3]}**. Every letter is processed, including e and s in grapes; the abbreviated grapes example in the original prompt omitted those two characters. Use `--word dodo` or omit it for a prompt.
