## 2. Longest sentence without A

Use `--interactive`. A loop rejects uppercase or lowercase A, tracks the longest accepted sentence and congratulates only a strictly longer record. `/quit` ends the game. Character length includes spaces as part of the sentence.

## 3. Paragraph statistics

The paragraph is the opening of Lewis Carroll’s public-domain Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland, linked in the source. The analysis reports characters, sentences, words, case-insensitive unique words, non-whitespace characters, average words per sentence and repeated occurrences beyond the first instance. Word tokens preserve internal apostrophes; sentence boundaries use . ! ?. These explicit rules make the counts reproducible. See [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11).
