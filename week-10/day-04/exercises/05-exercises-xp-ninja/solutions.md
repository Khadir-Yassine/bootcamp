## 1. Phone histories

Each Phone starts with its own call_history and messages lists. call prints and records caller/recipient. send_message delivers a dictionary with to/from/content to both phones so outgoing and incoming queries work. The three display methods filter outgoing messages, incoming messages and a selected sender. show_messages_from accepts a phone/number or prompts if omitted. The executable demo exchanges two messages and calls every display method.
